# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### Learning

**Learner**:
A person who has bought, or been given a seat on, a course and studies it.
_Avoid_: Student, User (for the person studying)

**Course Progress**:
Everything that records how far a learner has got through a course: which lessons they've started or completed, how far into each video they've watched, their quiz attempts, and whether they've completed the course. It is kept, never deleted, when a Refund Request is opened or a Refund is issued.
_Avoid_: Progress (on its own), Completion (for the whole record)

### People

**Admin**:
A member of Cadence staff who runs the platform. Only Admins decide Refund Requests and record what happened to the money.
_Avoid_: The team, Staff, Support (for the person deciding refunds); Team Admin (that's the person who manages a team licence)

### Refunds

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase in which a learner may raise a Refund Request. It doesn't stop while a Refund Request is open.
_Avoid_: Grace period, Cooling-off period

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it, and that decision is final. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet. While it is open, the learner's access to the course is paused, but their Course Progress is kept.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Withdrawn Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the learner took back before an Admin decided it. It has no Refund, their access to the course comes back straight away, and they may raise a new Refund Request later.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, Retracted

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the learner is told. It has no Refund, the learner's access to the course comes back, and they may raise a new Refund Request while the Refund Window is still open.
_Avoid_: Rejected

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund. Once the money has been sent, the learner's access to the course ends, but their Course Progress is kept and comes back if they buy the course again.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet. The learner's access to the course stays paused.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and the learner's access to the course stays paused. An Admin can try again, which makes it a Pending Refund once more.
