# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners, one at a time or as team seats.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a Purchase, with their reason in their own words. It is Requested until a person reviews it and either Approves or Declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
Money sent back to a learner for a Purchase. It exists only once a Refund Request is Approved, and the learner loses access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, Return

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, including the whole of the fourteenth day, during which a Refund Request can be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Policy**:
The conditions a Purchase must meet for a Refund Request: inside the Refund Window, under 2 hours watched, under half the lessons completed, and not a claimed team seat. A Refund is always the amount the learner paid.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, eligibility rules

**Chargeback**:
A charge reversed by the learner's bank through a card dispute, outside Cadence. It is never what a learner asks Cadence for.
